Our Story & Heritage

Where Culinary Craft Meets Botanical Poetry

“We don't merely serve dinner. We choreograph memories across plates, celebrating earth's most pristine seasonal harvests.”

Founded in 2012 by Executive Chef Julian Vance and Sommelier Claire Fontaine, AURA was born from a singular passion: elevating seasonal gastronomy through honest relationships with over 40 biodynamic growers, artisanal foragers, and coastal fishermen.

Every creation is prepared in our open exhibition kitchen, utilizing ancestral flame techniques, botanical infusions, and low-temperature reduction methods to honor each ingredient's authentic soul.

“True luxury in modern dining is not pretension—it is absolute freshness, intentional simplicity, and heartfelt hospitality.”
Chef Julian Vance — Executive Chef & Founder
